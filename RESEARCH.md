# Research log: what we studied, what we took, what we left

Date: 2026-10-31. Every technique in the engine traces back to one of these rows. When you add a technique, add a row.

## Open-source tools and repos

| Source | What it does | What we took | What we left, and why |
|---|---|---|---|
| OpenCV YuNet, `opencv/opencv_zoo` (Apache-2.0) | 230 KB face detector that runs on CPU through `cv2.FaceDetectorYN` | The detector in `tools/studio/track.py`. 33 ms per 960 px frame on a laptop CPU, no GPU, no MediaPipe, no model download at install (the file ships in `tools/studio/models/`) | Landmarks (not needed for a crop) |
| `aionixos/openclip`, `NaufalRizqullah/opensource-clipping` | Local-first clippers: MediaPipe face tracking with smooth pan, dead zone and anti-jitter; karaoke ASS captions; hook teaser; B-roll with Ken Burns; BGM ducking; speaker split-screen | Dead zone + smoothing idea; word-level karaoke in ASS; sidechain ducking (we already had it) | MediaPipe (needs libEGL on Linux and a 100 MB wheel), Gemini for picking moments (we rank moments with rules so no key is needed), Pexels B-roll (stock footage is not our look) |
| `fujiwarachoki/supoclip` PR 51 | "OpusClip-level" vertical pipeline: per-frame subject tracking with a critically damped spring, scene-aware layout (tracked crop for faces, blurred full-frame fit for slides), one decode pass for faces and cuts, single-pass encode, -14 LUFS | The critically damped spring (no overshoot), the one-pass analysis (faces and cuts from the same decoded frames), the -14 LUFS delivery | Emoji injection, crossfades at filler cuts (our cuts are hard, per the retention study below) |
| `KazKozDev/auto-vertical-reframe` (MIT) | PySceneDetect + YOLOv11 + ByteTrack + saliency; presets (`talking_head` zoom 1.05 to 1.85, `movie` locks one crop per shot) | The preset idea (talking head vs locked), zoom chosen per scene from subject size, snapping at cuts instead of panning across them | YOLO and PyTorch (700 MB of dependencies for a talking head that YuNet handles) |
| `videopython` FaceTrackingCrop | Compiles a smoothed face track to a per-frame ffmpeg `sendcmd` file: zero per-frame Python at render | The "compile the camera path, let ffmpeg run it" idea | `sendcmd` itself: per-frame crop commands made ffmpeg reconfigure the filter on every frame (a 20 s clip took over 7 minutes). We compile the path into crop `x`/`y` expressions instead; the same clip renders in 12 s |
| PySceneDetect `ContentDetector` | Scene cuts from frame-to-frame content change | The method (mean absolute difference on a thumbnail, threshold, minimum scene length) implemented inline so no second decode is needed | The package at render time (installed for `scenedetect` CLI use on B-roll libraries) |
| `wyattblue/auto-editor` (public domain) | Silence and dead-frame removal | The lesson from its issue tracker: cutting on a raw threshold clips word endings. Our in and out points sit on the measured pause, padded (`tools/studio/cut.py`) | The tool itself (we need word timing anyway) |
| `m-bain/whisperX` (BSD-2) | Whisper plus wav2vec2 forced alignment: true word boundaries instead of interpolated ones | Noted as the upgrade path when word timing drifts on fast speakers | Not installed: it pulls PyTorch. `faster-whisper` word timestamps are within about 40 ms on our takes, which the caption chunker tolerates |
| `Rikorose/DeepFilterNet` (MIT / Apache-2.0) | Full-band speech enhancement; ships a standalone `deep-filter` binary for macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel), Linux and Windows | Optional pre-pass before the voice chain (`--denoise deep`); `install.sh --deep-filter` fetches the binary and checks its size | Not on by default: the chain already handles the XTRA Muse takes, and the binary adds 28 MB |
| Remotion `@remotion/captions` `createTikTokStyleCaptions` | Groups word timestamps into pages by a maximum page duration | The page idea; our chunker also splits on punctuation, pauses and function words | Remotion itself: React plus Node for Owen is a support burden; HTML scenes rendered by Playwright (`tools/vsl/mg`) cover the motion graphics we need |
| `itsjwill/vanta` | A catalogue of integrations for an AI video engine (WhisperX, audio-separator, DeepFilterNet, auto-editor, Remotion captions, imgly background removal) | The shortlist | Everything behind an API key |
| Higgsfield `video-montage` workflow (their MCP, read 2026-10-31) | Their caption back end: transcribe on the cleanest audio, verify the transcript (similarity, words per second), burn one look, verify the burn from the encoded file; caption clearance with protected regions and contact-sheet inspection; at most 5 words / 32 characters per caption; minimum font 2 % of frame height; portrait margins top 10 %, bottom 17 % | The four gates (transcribe, verify, burn, verify) as `review.py` checks; face clearance measured from the track; "a transcript is not proof of a correct burn" (the contact sheet is mandatory) | Their fixed bottom placement (the measured talking-head band is higher, see below) |
| Higgsfield `video-editing` workflow (Higgsedit) | A native Node compositor: frames, keyframes, staggered cards, word-by-word text arrival, counters, masks, 2.5D camera | The motion vocabulary (enter / settle / exit, stagger, word arrival, reveal by mask) as the checklist for `tools/vsl/mg/scenes.html` | The compositor: closed, sandbox-only |

## Editing references (what the best editors actually do)

| Source | Findings we encoded |
|---|---|
| writepanda.ai, "The 7 laws of retention editing" (22 videos measured, July 2026) | Hook within 2.5 s (shorts) or 10 s (long-form); something visible changes every 3 to 8 s; payoff at 55 to 85 %; burned captions in shorts only, 1 to 6 words, one emphasis mechanism; keep fillers, cut between beats; graphics own the top 40 %, captions the 48 to 66 % band, nothing covers a face; end on the payoff, no outro. These are the checks in `tools/studio/review.py` |
| writepanda.ai, "The Hormozi caption style, measured" | Modern Hormozi: zero B-roll, 100 % face time, 1 to 4 words per screen (median 3), mixed case, weight for emphasis, semantic cuts every 3.5 to 5.6 s alternating wide and tight (about 1.5x), slow drift on holds over 8 s, numbered chapter pill for listicles, no CTA. Encoded as the `clean` caption style, the punch-in pattern and the pill |
| choppity.com, Hormozi style page | The 2021 look: caps, yellow active word, 10 to 20 % punch-ins, speaker fills 60 % of frame, cuts every 1 to 3 s, zero dead air. Encoded as the `impact` caption style |
| TomsProject, "Alex Hormozi's Edit Style Deconstructed" (YouTube y60ohM4nr4Y, transcript) | One idea every 3 to 7 s; a B-cam hides cuts; the hook sits on screen for the first 2 to 4 s as native Instagram text; captions near 51 % of frame height because of the app UI; fonts Proxima Nova and Instrument Serif; yellow, white, blue; long-form uses a drawn look (handwritten font, dotted lines, paint-on and linear-wipe reveals, trim paths) with a subtle glow |
| "The Complete Guide to Editing like Iman Gadzhi in 2026" (YouTube O6HOYu_ZEnw, transcript) | The 60-30-10 colour rule from one coloured light; two fonts (a sans for everything, a serif or script "special" font for the one word that matters); rounded rectangles with 30 to 70 px corners and a glowing edge; four animation types (3D text write-outs, abstract story scenes, cartoon character scenes, UI pop-ups); the camera never stops (start the next move before the last ends, "null stacking"); text pops stepped to 15 fps for a cinematic feel; blurred, darkened vignette behind text. Encoded as the `serif` caption style, the hook card, the card tokens in `tools/vsl/mg/BRAND.md` |
| "How to Edit Videos Like NELK" (YouTube n3FS2en3HdE, transcript) | Teal-and-orange grade with letterbox bars on montages; everything cut to the beat; the drop uses three sounds (build-up, a bang, a bass boom); montage ends with a smash to black and the next scene opens on an establishing drone shot; subtitles in Futura heavy italic with a red and blue drop shadow (RGB split); a sliding "pop tag" when a new person appears; mood music under every scene. Encoded as the `cinematic` grade, the synthesized riser and hit, the montage recipe in `EDITING_GRAMMAR.md` |
| Meta Instagram Reels ad specs; Google Ads vertical safe zones; TikTok in-feed template (measured by upload-post.com) | The safe zones in `tools/studio/platforms.py` |

## Long-form engineering notes (2026-10-31, second pass)

| Decision | Why |
|---|---|
| Cutaways (chapter cards, quote cards, B-roll) are composited over the finished footage base with `overlay` enabled between timestamps, each cutaway clip shifted with `setpts` and faded on its alpha | one encode, the voice never stops, and a cutaway can be added or moved without re-rendering the pieces |
| Keyword pops instead of burned captions on long formats (`captions: pops`) | the 502-frame study found zero burned captions on top long-form channels; pops at the spoken word carry emphasis |
| A visible layout change at least every `change_every_s` (40 s for YouTube, 30 s for VSL and lessons) is a review check, and B-roll fills the gaps at sentence ends | the measured 20 to 40 s alternation rule; the engine reports every gap it could not fill |
| Chapter titles must be written by a person (`--chapters auto` only finds the boundaries and the review flags placeholder titles) | a chapter title is a promise; a transcript fragment is not |
| Thumbnail and cover from the best face frame (largest face, highest detector score, inside the first 8 s) with 2 to 4 words in the caption face on a dark gradient band | thumbnail rules in the YouTube sources in FORMATS.md; the band keeps the text readable on bright footage |

## What is still open

- Forced alignment (WhisperX) for speakers faster than about 4 words per second.
- Speaker diarization and split-screen for two-person podcasts (pyannote needs a token; we skipped it).
- A blurred full-frame fit for slide and screen-recording scenes inside a reel (the standalone `reframe` handles faces; slides get the static crop for now).
- The Nelk montage builder (beat detection on the music, cut list to the beat) is a recipe, not yet a command.

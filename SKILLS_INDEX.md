# Skills in this kit

Say what you want in plain words; Claude picks the skill. This list is for curiosity.

## Video
- **reel-editor**: raw clip in, finished reel(s) out for any platform (analysis, cut, tracked camera, captions, hook, end card, voice, grade, loudness, checks).
- **auto-reframe**: any clip to 9:16, 4:5 or 1:1 with a face-tracked virtual camera.
- **caption-styles**: the four caption looks, placement, the hook card, keyword pops, the listicle pill.
- **platform-formatter**: sizes, safe zones, length limits, loudness and exports per platform.
- **edit-grammar**: the house editing brain (the measured laws, the three dialects, the beat-map process).
- **cold-review**: an unbiased second viewer that returns SHIP, FIX FIRST or DOG SHIT with timecodes.
- **vsl-producer**: long-form sales videos with animated cutaways, checks and a cold review.
- **video-use**: a general video-understanding helper (transcribe, find moments, describe footage).

## Brand

# Closer mentality

## Who is on the other side

**Become The Closer:** a commission-only closer already on the phones this week, whose income moves with their skill.
The site says it plainly: not for people who want a pre-recorded course, leads handed to them, or who think the problem
is the market, the price or the prospects. The pain in their words: "I want to think about it" is stealing their
commissions. Most closers never see 350K; the skill they have today is their ceiling. They are one of four types (The
Grinder, The Technician, The Dreamer, The Architect) and the quiz names which.

**Set More Annuities:** a life, mortgage protection or health insurance agent selling $100 to $180 a month policies to
working-class clients. The money is already in their book: clients who said "I have a 401(k)" on a call they already
made. The pain in the docs' words: they "keep dialing new leads for $140-a-month term policies", the client "sits in your
CRM collecting dust", "and you never asked." Positioning: "Stop buying leads. The money is already in your book."

## How we sell (the frame)

Chase and Owen teach one frame, The Way, four moves the prospect makes: **See** (they say the real reason themselves),
**Own** (they agree out loud it is costing them), **Lead** (a true story and what it takes, said detached), **Decide**
(the close takes care of itself). Objectionless selling is objection prevention, not objection handling: an objection
at the close is a move that was skipped earlier. For the coaching detail on any move, use the Become The Closer
connector live; do not paste course material into files.

Every piece of content runs the same four moves on the viewer:

| Move | In a reel or VSL |
|---|---|
| See | the hook is their sentence, not ours ("I want to think about it is stealing your commissions") |
| Own | the stakes in numbers they already know (commission earned and given back; the policy they sold once) |
| Lead | one true mechanism, shown, detached ("set it, we close it, you get paid"; "two or three simple questions") |
| Decide | one ask, said once, low pressure ("Take the closer quiz"; "Create your free account") |

## The angle the owner chose (2026-10-30)

- Other programs only ask agents to set appointments. We also teach them to close themselves. Say both; let them pick
  ("sell or set"). Most end up setting anyway, because they do not want to do the close.
- Make them feel left out of money other agents already make. Proof for that, and only this proof until more is
  filmed: 20+ agents, $3.8M placed in 2025.
- Everything on the Set More Annuities side is free to the agent. Become The Closer is paid mentorship; never call it
  free, never promise a result.

## What we never say

- "Guarantee" or "guaranteed" about a result, a return or growth. The one exception is an insurance product feature
  stated exactly as the carrier states it.
- "Free", "no cost" about Become The Closer.
- "Annuity" in anything a client (not an agent) will read; use accumulation-to-preservation and self-funded pension
  language.
- A number without a source. The docs give $3,000 to $8,000 per case; a spoken "$30,000" stays spoken and never goes on
  screen until it is sourced.
- Cross-selling annuities on a Medicare appointment (health agents). Roth content without "general information, not
  tax advice".
- Em dashes, the rule of three, and the banned words in `HOUSE_RULES.md`.

## Honesty gates

No faked stories, results, scarcity or busyness. No urgency built on illness or death. Never skip fees, splits or
surrender periods when they come up. Never pressure someone who says they cannot afford it. Any result on screen carries
the site's own line: individual experiences, results vary, no promise of income.

## Tone

Second person, blunt, never cruel. Name what they did right first. One thing to fix at a time. Sounds like a real person
texting, never corporate. The viewer should feel seen, then called out, then shown the move.

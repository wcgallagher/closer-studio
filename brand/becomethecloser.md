# Become The Closer: brand sheet for video

Read from becomethecloser.com on 2026-10-30 (Firecrawl branding scrape, confidence 0.99). Use these values for every
reel, VSL card, end card and thumbnail made for the company.

## Colour

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| background | `#0C0806` | the site's near-black, warm. Cards, end cards, graphics backgrounds |
| text | `#EFEAE2` | all text on dark, captions |
| accent | `#F3680F` | the ember orange: buttons, the highlighted caption word, hook lines, strikes, live dots |
| secondary | `#2E2017` | panels, quiet surfaces, dividers |

No other accent colours. No pure white. The site's hero image is an ember / aura on black: warm glow, never neon.

## Type

| Role | Face | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Headlines | Fraunces (serif) | sentence case, tight tracking, as on the site's "I want to think about it is stealing your commissions." |
| Body and buttons | Inter | buttons are square (0 px radius), orange with near-black text |
| Labels | JetBrains Mono | uppercase, tracked, for eyebrows like "Become The Closer / Film 01" |
| Captions on footage | Montserrat Black, uppercase | the video layer only, never inside a card |

Radius: 0 px on buttons, about 9 px on cards.

## Voice on screen (the site's own lines, use them as they are)

- "I want to think about it" is stealing your commissions.
- Objectionless selling is objection prevention, not objection handling.
- There are only four types of closers: The Grinder, The Technician, The Dreamer, The Architect.
- Most closers never see 350K. The skill they have today is their ceiling.
- The easy deals will not tell you if you can actually sell. The hard ones will.
- Every "I need to think about it" is commission you earned and gave back.
- Call to action everywhere: Take the closer quiz (becomethecloser.com/quiz).

## What the site says it is not

Not a pre-recorded course. Not leads handed to you. Not for people who blame the market, the price or the prospects.
Reels that promise any of those break the brand.

## Compliance line the site uses under testimonials

"These are individual experiences. Results vary, and nothing here is a promise of income or of any particular result."
Any reel that shows a result carries the same idea.

# House rules

Every skill in this repo follows these when it writes or edits for us. Where a skill's own instructions disagree, these win.

## Skills

- No skill file without an `AUDIT.md`: every figure, path and tool sourced. Run `skills/core/zero-context-auditor/scripts/audit_skill.py` before committing a skill.

## Copy

- No em dashes. Use commas, periods or parentheses.
- No rule of three ("fast, simple, and powerful"). Use two or four.
- Banned words: delve, tapestry, testament, pivotal, landscape, realm, underscore, showcase, seamless, robust, unlock, elevate, navigate (as a metaphor), leverage (as a verb), "in today's world", "it's not just X, it's Y".
- Name feelings plainly. Vary sentence length hard.
- Never claim what the data can't back up. No invented stats, testimonials or income promises.
- Client-facing annuity copy never says "annuity". Use accumulation-to-preservation and self-funded pension language. Agent-facing copy may say it.

## Video

- Audio and picture stay in sync to the frame. Prove it by measuring, not by eyeballing.
- Every cut is a decision: dead air, restarts and flubs go. Punch-ins (100% to about 112%) on hooks, turns and proof.
- Captions match what was said, word for word, and never cover the face.
- Before anything ships, a second agent reviews the final render with fresh eyes.

## Media buying

- Decisions come from the account's own numbers, not industry averages, unless the account has no history.
- Every recommendation names the metric it moves and how it will be measured.
